# Third-party materials

The MIT License in [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE) covers Quotengine source code and original
documentation authored for this project.

Third-party datasets, radiographs, photographs and other evaluation assets are not
automatically relicensed under MIT. They remain subject to their respective source
licenses, permissions and provenance requirements, as recorded in
[`eval/datasets/registry.json`](./eval/datasets/registry.json) and the individual case
manifests.

This public repository contains only the six evaluation images sourced from the CC BY case reports
listed below. Their figure files, captions and article licenses were verified against the official
Europe PMC article packages on 2026-08-03; the files are reproduced unchanged. Preserve the source,
article, figure and license attribution whenever redistributing or updating the fixture set.
